Why browse the stacks?
Books on one subject sit together
Call numbers group books by subject, so the shelf around one good book is full of related ones.
You find books keywords miss
A great book may not use your search words in its title. On the shelf you’ll see it anyway.
You can judge a book quickly
Flip through the table of contents and index to see if it really fits, before you check it out.
Online works too
Many catalog records let you browse nearby call numbers on screen when you can’t get to the library.
